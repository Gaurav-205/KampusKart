# Code of Conduct

KampusKart follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page summarises it; the linked text is the authoritative version.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming, including to newcomers and first-time contributors.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on scope, assignment and priorities.
- Credit other people's work. Don't copy another contributor's open pull request and submit it as your own.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory or discriminatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Spam, including mass low-effort issues or pull requests, repeated "please assign me" pings, and AI-generated reports that don't reflect the actual code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, unassign work, or block participants who break this code.

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@Gaurav-205](https://github.com/Gaurav-205), through GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
